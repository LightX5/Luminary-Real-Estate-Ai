# Luminary — White-Label AI Customer Service & Sales Agent

AI that works while you sleep.

Luminary is a white-label AI customer service and business automation platform that helps businesses handle customer conversations, answer questions, qualify leads, and automate repetitive support tasks 24/7.

The system can be customized for different businesses and industries by changing the company configuration, branding, knowledge base, and AI instructions.

Built for businesses that want to provide faster customer support without having to scale their support team for every increase in customer volume.

---

## 🚀 What Luminary Can Do

Depending on the client's configuration, Luminary can:

- Answer customer questions 24/7
- Respond to frequently asked questions
- Provide product and service information
- Qualify leads
- Collect customer information
- Recommend products or services
- Handle common customer-support requests
- Guide customers through simple processes
- Capture inquiries for human follow-up
- Escalate complex issues to a human
- Maintain a consistent brand voice
- Provide instant responses outside business hours

Luminary is designed to work as a **first-line AI employee**, handling repetitive conversations while human employees focus on more complex tasks.

## 🏢 Built for Different Businesses

Luminary can be customized for:

- 🛒 E-commerce
- 🏠 Real Estate
- 🏦 Financial Services
- 🏥 Healthcare & Clinics
- 🎓 Education
- 🏨 Hotels & Hospitality
- 🚚 Logistics
- 💻 SaaS & Technology
- 📱 Telecommunications
- 💼 Professional Services
